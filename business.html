Methodology

How I would examine a business idea

A practical process derived from the kinds of work covered in my Waseda business-related coursework. The sequence is a draft to refine through projects and real-world feedback.

01

Discover

Identify users, context, real pain points and the jobs they need done.

02

Propose

Express the unique value and a concrete product or service promise.

03

Model

Specify customers, payer, channels, costs and revenue logic.

04

Calculate

Draft financial assumptions, unit economics and key viability risks.

05

Validate

Test assumptions, revise the story and communicate it clearly.

Decision rule

A convincing slide deck is not proof of demand. Distinguish observed evidence, assumptions and projections before asserting that a business will work.

01 / Problem brief

One-page customer-need analysis

Business Model Canvas, revenue/cost logic and a simple financial scenario with explicit assumptions.
